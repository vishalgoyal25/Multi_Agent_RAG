# Northbay Commerce AI — Contract & Trial Terms

This document summarizes the standard commercial terms that apply to a
Northbay Commerce AI subscription agreement. It is provided for reference;
the signed order form and master services agreement govern in case of any
discrepancy with this summary.

## Trial period

New customers are entitled to a **30-day evaluation period** from the
contract start date, during which the customer may cancel without
liability for the subscription fee. Any professional services (including
Forward-Deployed Engineer time) delivered during the evaluation period are
billed separately and are not covered by the evaluation cancellation
right.

## Contract term and renewal

Standard subscription agreements run for an initial term of 12 months and
renew automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party
provides written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the current
term ends.

## Payment terms

Subscription fees are invoiced annually in advance. Invoices are due within
30 days of the invoice date. Add-on charges (additional data sources,
additional FDE weeks) are invoiced monthly in arrears, based on actual
usage during the prior month.

## Termination for cause

Either party may terminate the agreement for the other party's uncured
material breach, following 30 days' written notice and an opportunity to
cure. Northbay's standard agreement does not include a termination-for-
convenience clause outside the evaluation period described above; a
customer wishing to exit before the end of a contract term outside the
evaluation window should contact their account team to discuss options.

## Data return on termination

Upon termination, Northbay retains a copy of the customer's configuration
and connected-source mappings for 30 days to support a possible reactivation,
after which all customer data is permanently deleted from Northbay-managed
storage. Data residing in the customer's own cloud account (see the
security and data handling document) is unaffected, since Northbay Studio
runs inside that account rather than a separate Northbay-hosted environment.
