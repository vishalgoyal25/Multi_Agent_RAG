# Northbay Commerce AI — Agentic Template Catalogue

Northbay ships pre-built agent templates across four business functions.
Each template is a starting configuration — tools, data source
expectations, and a baseline playbook — that a customer's Agentic AI
Development engagement then customizes.

## Marketing templates

- **Campaign Brief Agent** — drafts a campaign brief from a short prompt
  and the customer's brand guidelines, pulling past campaign performance
  data to suggest a channel mix.
- **Content Localization Agent** — adapts existing marketing copy for a
  new region or language, flagging claims that may need legal review
  before publishing.
- **Promotion Calendar Agent** — proposes a promotional calendar that
  avoids conflicts with existing planned promotions and known low-inventory
  periods.

## Merchandising templates

- **Assortment Planning Agent** — recommends which SKUs to add or drop
  from a category based on sell-through data and category performance
  trends.
- **Pricing Exception Agent** — flags SKUs whose current price has drifted
  outside the customer's configured margin bands, for human review.
- **Markdown Timing Agent** — suggests when to begin markdowns on slow-
  moving inventory, based on historical sell-through curves.

## Customer experience (CX) templates

- **Ticket Triage Agent** — classifies incoming support tickets by urgency
  and routes them to the correct queue.
- **Returns Reason Agent** — analyzes return reason codes across a product
  line to surface recurring quality or sizing issues.
- **Proactive Outreach Agent** — identifies customers likely to churn based
  on order pattern changes and drafts a retention outreach message for
  human approval.

## B2B sales templates

- **Account Research Agent** — compiles a briefing on a prospective B2B
  account ahead of a sales call, drawing on public information and any
  prior interaction history in the CRM.
- **RFP Response Agent** — drafts a first-pass response to a request for
  proposal using the customer's existing product documentation.
- **Renewal Risk Agent** — flags B2B accounts approaching renewal that show
  declining usage, for the account team to prioritize.

## Choosing a starting template

Most customers start with two to three templates in their first quarter,
typically one from CX and one from either Marketing or Merchandising,
rather than deploying across all four functions simultaneously. Northbay's
own guidance is that a template deployed well in one function builds
internal trust faster than several templates deployed thinly across many
functions at once.
