# Northbay Commerce AI — Pricing & Tiers

Northbay offers three pricing tiers. All tiers include access to Northbay
Studio and at least one agentic template category; the tiers differ in
template count, data source limits, and support level.

## Starter

- Up to 2 agentic templates active at once
- Up to 3 connected data sources
- Standard email support (2 business day response target)
- Includes a **14-day free trial**
- Best fit for a single-department pilot (e.g. one CX template)

## Growth

- Up to 6 agentic templates active at once
- Up to 10 connected data sources
- Priority support (see the support & SLA policy document for response
  time targets)
- Includes a **14-day free trial**
- Includes one Forward-Deployed Engineer engagement of up to 8 weeks
- Best fit for a multi-department rollout across 2–3 business functions

## Enterprise

- Unlimited agentic templates
- Unlimited connected data sources
- Dedicated FDE embed for the full engagement duration
- Custom SLA, negotiated per account
- Custom pricing — enterprise accounts are quoted individually based on
  deployment scale and are not published on a fixed price list

## Upgrading between tiers

A customer can upgrade from Starter to Growth, or Growth to Enterprise, at
any point in their contract term. Upgrades take effect at the start of the
next billing cycle and do not require a new trial period, since the trial
applies to first-time adoption of the platform rather than to each
individual tier.

## Add-ons available on any tier

- Additional connected data sources beyond a tier's included limit, billed
  per source per month
- Additional FDE weeks beyond what a tier includes, billed at Northbay's
  standard FDE day rate
