# Northbay Commerce AI — Service Catalogue

Northbay sells five distinct services. Customers can buy any single service
on its own, though most enterprise engagements start with AI Strategy and
expand into the others over the following two quarters.

## 1. AI Strategy

A 4–6 week engagement where a Northbay strategist works with the customer's
leadership to identify which business processes are the highest-impact
candidates for agentic AI, and to build a prioritized 12-month roadmap.
Output is a written roadmap document plus a short list of pilot use cases.

## 2. AI Studio Deployment

Standing up the Northbay Studio platform itself inside the customer's
chosen cloud environment, connecting the first data sources, and
configuring baseline governance policies. This is typically the first
technical milestone after AI Strategy concludes.

## 3. Agentic AI Development

Building and tuning custom agents for the customer's specific workflows,
starting from Northbay's pre-built templates (see the agentic template
catalogue) and adapting them — new tools, new data sources, adjusted
approval thresholds — to match how the customer's team actually operates.

## 4. FDE-Led Execution

Embedding a Northbay Forward-Deployed Engineer with the customer's team for
the duration of the rollout. The FDE's job is to close the gap between "the
agent works in a demo" and "the agent is trusted in production" — this
includes handling edge cases the initial build did not anticipate,
training the customer's internal team, and staying engaged until the
customer's own staff can operate the deployment independently.

## 5. Integration & Governance

Ongoing service covering new connector development, governance policy
updates, and audit support as the customer's Northbay deployment grows
past its initial scope.

## How the services combine

A typical first-year engagement runs AI Strategy, then AI Studio
Deployment and Agentic AI Development in parallel, with an FDE embedded
throughout. Integration & Governance becomes the primary ongoing service
once the initial rollout is stable, usually starting in month four or
five.
